Each reply moves the drill on one stage. After 25 seconds of silence the scammer sends one ISOLATION nudge, which doesn't advance the stage (at most one per stage). If the AI is slower than about 8 seconds or fails, the stage's canned line is used. After 3 failures in a row, "Running in offline practice mode" is shown. ?demo=1 runs the drill fully on canned lines.

Safety guard (in the browser, before anything is sent)

Checked in order ENDS AS A LOSS
  1. The exact fake OTP → "Shared the OTP"
  2. Always blocked: an Aadhaar pattern (12 digits), a card number (13–19 digits), a PAN (ABCDE1234F), or "PIN", "CVV", "OTP" or "code" followed by digits
  3. A 4–6 digit number that looks like a code: (a) at stage 5 or later, when the reply is mostly just the number, or (b) next to OTP, code, PIN, password or CVV at any stage

Always on
  • A permanent training strip
  • Fictional officer, fake OTP, fake account number
  • The scammer's instructions framed as a consented training simulation
  • Nothing kept after the session; API key never in the repo

Boundary

Deferred NOT NOW
  • Hindi / Hinglish: needs translated canned lines, tips and interface text
  • Grandparent-scam scenario: polish one script first
  • Voice: latency and reliability risk
  • Link to another phone: needs hosting and shared state
  • Drill history: needs storage, against "store nothing"
Non-goals
  • Accounts, login, databases
  • Real calls or SMS
  • AI-generated safety advice
  • The AI judging the outcome
  • Collecting any real personal or financial data
  • A game-show feel (points, leaderboards, confetti)

Decisions made at review

  • The safety guard was narrowed: sensitive patterns are always blocked, short numbers only when they look like a code
  • A "Your name" field for the child in Setup
  • Safe contact = a name and an optional relation, so the button reads "Call Rahul (son)"
  • The bank is free text ("e.g. SBI, HDFC")
  • The scammer says "[Parent name] ji". No gender field.
